# Design: learned reputation without a reputation score
**Date:** 2026-10-03
**Repo:** `N-T-SH/learned-reputation`
**Status:** Frozen baselines exist. Training has not started. This replaces the earlier standing sketch for the current run.

## Question

Does a trained policy use visible history, and messages, when it decides who to nominate, and does that differ from the same model frozen?

Reputation here is that pattern. It is not a number in the prompt, and it is not a label the environment computes.

## Game

Eight seats. Each has one unit. A seat contributes between 0 and 1. The pot is the sum of contributions inside the working set, multiplied by 1.6, then split equally among those seats. A seat that is out gets 0.8, no matter what it offered.

A pair forms only if each names the other. Naming yourself counts as staying available. A one-sided nomination is not a pair. If nobody pairs, one seat is kept at random so the round is not empty.

The individual payoff, if in, is the unspent part of the unit plus the share of the pot. Full contribution by everyone pays 1.6 each. Full withholding pays 1.0 each. A seat that withholds while others contribute can do better than either, until the others stop naming it. Isolation at 0.8 is worse than staying in a contributing group. That gap is the channel. The pot does not pay anyone for being reputable.

## What a seat sees

Only the last working set: those seats' previous contributions, including its own if it was in, plus messages addressed to it from the previous round. No population score. No game-theory words in the prompt. The words reputation, exclude, free-rider, public good, Nash, defect, and cooperate are rejected by the prompt check.

The model replies with a message, a list of partner ids, and a contribution. The reply enters the environment only through repair. Illegal ids are dropped. A reply that is not valid JSON fails closed: empty message, no nominations, contribution 0.

## What is locked

Temperature 0.4. Reasoning off. Eight seats, twenty rounds, five episodes for the comparison file. A trained run must use the same model, the same temperature, and reasoning off. The before picture for a Qwen 3.8 27B pilot is `runs/pgg/frozen_qwen_qwen3.8-27b_choice_s8_t0.4_seed0_n5.jsonl`. The Qwen3 8B file is a separate baseline.

## Reward, if training happens

The reward is the payoff the environment already writes. It is not a point for being included, and it is not the group's total. Inclusion-only would treat a full contributor and a free rider as the same. The group total is higher under full contribution, but the trainer does not optimize the group total.

## What would count as a result

Training changed nominations relative to the frozen file: low contributions are named less often, and the seats who remain in contribute more than in the frozen file. A frozen habit that leaves seats out at random is not that result. A second game is a later check, not a substitute reward.

## Out of scope

Hardcoded reputation. An exclude verb. Reasoning-mode training. An exploitation probe. A claim of emergence from frozen logs. T3 until T2 is marked finished.
