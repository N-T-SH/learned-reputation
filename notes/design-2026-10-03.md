# Design: learned reputation without a reputation score
**Date:** 2026-10-03, updated after review
**Repo:** `N-T-SH/learned-reputation`
**Status:** Frozen baselines exist. Do not train until the ledger rerun exists.

## Question

Does a trained policy use visible history when it decides who to nominate, and does that differ from the same model frozen?

## What the review changed

The old prompt showed only the last working set. An outsider was invisible, so exclusion could not track that seat's contribution, and reintegration could not be observed. The prompt now carries a public ledger: the last five rounds of every seat's contribution and nominations.

A lone seat used to earn 1.6 times its own contribution. That seat now gets the isolation payoff, 0.8. A one-seat pot is not a group.

Seat labels in the prompt are shuffled every episode. The log keeps the real ids. The 27B file already shows position bias: seat 0 was out in 12 of 100 rounds, seat 7 in 83.

The training reward, when a pilot happens, is the seat's return from that round to the end of the episode, not that round's payoff alone. A same-round payoff pays withholding and never sees the later drop.

Stay at eight seats until the ledger rerun exists. Sixteen seats is a later robustness check, not the next file.

## Game

Eight seats. Each has one unit. Contribution is between 0 and 1. The pot is the sum inside a working set of at least two, multiplied by 1.6, split equally. Anyone out, and anyone alone, gets 0.8.

A pair forms only if each names the other. One-sided nomination is not a pair.

## What a seat sees

The last five rounds: every seat's contribution and nominations, in shuffled label order. Messages from the previous round addressed to it. No population score. No game-theory words in the prompt. The prompt does not say how many rounds remain.

## What would count as a result

After the ledger rerun, a scripted free rider who always contributes 0 is named less often than seats who contribute. A trained policy, scored on return-to-go, names that seat less often than the frozen policy, and the seats who remain in contribute more. The current 27B file cannot support that claim.

## Out of scope for the next file

A second game. Sixteen seats. T3. A claim of emergence from the current logs.
