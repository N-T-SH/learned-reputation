# Pre-registration, 20 Oct 2026 (committed before any run)

Claim: Frozen LLM agents in a partner-choice public-goods game readmit a reformed free rider when its bad rounds leave memory, not by recognizing reform. With the
full record, readmission is slow or absent.

P1 (unforgiving): with full history, the reformer is never readmitted by round 24.
P3 (forgetting): with window-5, readmission latency is 6 rounds or fewer after the reform; with full history it is at least twice that, in both seeds.
P4 (inertia): with window-5, the forgiveness gap is above 0 after round 14.

T1 record vs recency: logistic regression named(i->j,t) ~ c_j,t-1 + mean(c_j,t-5..t-2) + episode fixed effects. "Uses longer record" = second coefficient > 0, interval
excludes 0, in window-5 and full arms; that coefficient is about 0 in window-1.
T2 latency: rounds after the reform until at least 2 of 5 model seats name the reformer in 2 consecutive rounds.
T3 partner choice: mean contribution of model seats, choice arms vs fixed-group arm.
Prediction: higher with choice.
T4 probes: P(named | good record, bad last move) >= P(named | bad record, good last move) in both models and all three framings = record tracking.
Leading Eight probe: justification = a good-record seat that refused a bad partner is named about as often as one that included a good partner; apology = a bad-record seat that just included a good partner is named more than one that did not.

Reporting: every episode is its own row; 2 seeds = preview; no pooled p-values.
